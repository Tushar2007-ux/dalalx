Clipped-notice alert with four severities: info, notice, warning, critical.

```jsx
<AlertNotice severity="critical" title="Spoofer suspended" reference="SV-0412" time="14:32:07">…</AlertNotice>
```

All colours come from the nearest `[data-edition]`; wrap screens in `<Edition>`. Numbers always use `.dx-num` (IBM Plex Mono, tabular). Buy/sell always pair colour with ▲/▼ and a word.
