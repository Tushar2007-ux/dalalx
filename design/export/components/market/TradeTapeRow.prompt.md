One printed trade on the tape; new rows typeset in left to right.

```jsx
<TradeTapeRow time="14:32:07.412" symbol="RELNC" side="buy" price={2481.5} qty={120} buyer="Whale" seller="Munim" fresh />
```

All colours come from the nearest `[data-edition]`; wrap screens in `<Edition>`. Numbers always use `.dx-num` (IBM Plex Mono, tabular). Buy/sell always pair colour with ▲/▼ and a word.
