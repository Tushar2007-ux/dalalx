Live headline strip under the masthead; new headlines typeset in.

```jsx
<HeadlineBanner kicker="Stop Press" state="breaking" tone="down" headline="RELNC slides 4% as Panic Seller dumps 12,000 shares" time="14:32:07" />
```

All colours come from the nearest `[data-edition]`; wrap screens in `<Edition>`. Numbers always use `.dx-num` (IBM Plex Mono, tabular). Buy/sell always pair colour with ▲/▼ and a word.
